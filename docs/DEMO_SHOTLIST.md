# PatientTriage.ai — Demo Shot List (60–90 seconds)

Goal: make the decide-vs-recommend boundary and the Mass Casualty fallback
both **visually obvious**, even to someone watching with the sound off.

| # | Shot | What to show | Why it matters |
|---|------|--------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Cold open on a low-risk patient** | Enter scenario #1 from the test checklist (twisted ankle). Click Run. Let the ESI 5 (blue) badge land. | Establishes the baseline calmly before you show something urgent — the contrast is the whole point. |
| 2 | **Cut to the cardiac emergency** | Clear the form, enter scenario #2 (crushing chest pain, HR 135, SpO2 90%). Click Run. | This is your money shot — a visibly red ESI 1–2 badge appearing. |
| 3 | **Hold on the decision panel** | Pause 2 full seconds on the "DECISION: Auto-order ECG + Cardiac Enzyme Panel" line before moving on. Don't talk over it — let it read. | This is the "decide" half of decide-vs-recommend. Give it room to land. |
| 4 | **Pan to the explainability panel** | Point at (or circle, if editing) the three progress bars — vitals, NLP urgency, age — while the cardiac case is still on screen. | Answers "why did it say that" before anyone has to ask. This is your credibility beat. |
| 5 | **Flip Mass Casualty Mode on, live** | With the same cardiac patient still loaded, toggle Mass Casualty Mode ON on camera. Show the red banner appear and the panel instantly re-route to a rule-based result with no explainability bars. | Demonstrates the fallback isn't a separate demo — it's a live re-route of the same patient, which is the actual architectural claim. |
| 6 | **Close on the recommend line** | Zoom or highlight the small "Recommended — nurse confirms" text under the ESI badge. | Last thing the viewer sees should reinforce the core design principle, not just the tech. |

**Pacing note:** shots 2–4 are your core 30 seconds — don't rush them.
Shots 1 and 6 can be quick (3–5 seconds each); they're framing, not content.

**If you're narrating live instead of editing after the fact:** this
shot order matches the Layer 1→4 walkthrough in the video script almost
exactly — reuse those lines, just point at the running app instead of the
static architecture PDF.
